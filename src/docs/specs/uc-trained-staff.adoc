== UC-TS1: Review a Questionnaire

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| ID | UC-TS1
| Goal | Review a questionnaire submitted by a nurse
| Actor-Goal Ref | TS-1, TS-2, TS-3, TS-4
| Level | User Goal
| Primary Actor | Trained Staff
|===

NOTE: This use case covers the review process and its three possible outcomes: approve (TS-2), reject (TS-3), and request changes (TS-4).
Each outcome is a branch of the same user goal: the trained staff member reviews a questionnaire and decides its fate.

=== Stakeholders and Interests

Trained Staff:: Wants to verify clinical correctness and FHIR mapping quality before a questionnaire reaches patients.
Nurse:: Wants timely, clear feedback on submitted questionnaires.
Patient (indirect):: Benefits from reviewed, high-quality questionnaires.
Hospital:: Wants a quality gate between creation and publication.

=== Trigger

A questionnaire in Review status appears on the trained staff member's dashboard.

=== Preconditions

. A questionnaire exists in Review status.
. Automated quality checks have already run (during submission, UC-N4).

=== Main Success Scenario (Approve)

. The trained staff member opens the review dashboard.
. The trained staff member selects a questionnaire in Review status.
. The system displays the questionnaire: source, rendered preview, and automated quality check results. _(SYS-3)_
. The trained staff member reviews the clinical content (question wording, options, descriptions).
. The trained staff member reviews the FHIR mappings (LOINC codes, SNOMED codes, value types).
. The trained staff member reviews the automated quality check results.
. The trained staff member approves the questionnaire.
. The system transitions the questionnaire to Approved status.

=== Extensions

*7a. The trained staff member requests changes.*::
  7a.1. The trained staff member writes feedback describing the required changes.
  7a.2. The system transitions the questionnaire back to Draft status.
  7a.3. The feedback is attached to the questionnaire and visible to the nurse (UC-N3, extension 2a).

*7b. The trained staff member rejects the questionnaire.*::
  7b.1. The trained staff member writes a rejection reason.
  7b.2. The system transitions the questionnaire to Rejected status.
  7b.3. The rejection reason is attached to the questionnaire.

*3a. The trained staff member wants to preview the questionnaire as a patient.*::
  3a.1. The system renders the questionnaire in patient preview mode (same rendering as UC-N2).
  3a.2. The trained staff member interacts with the preview.
  3a.3. The trained staff member returns to the review screen.

*6a. An automated quality check has failed.*::
  6a.1. The system highlights the failed check.
  6a.2. The trained staff member may still approve if they judge the failure acceptable, or request changes.

=== Postconditions

Success Guarantee (Approve):: The questionnaire is in Approved status.
Success Guarantee (Request Changes):: The questionnaire is in Draft status with feedback attached.
Success Guarantee (Reject):: The questionnaire is in Rejected status with a reason attached.
Minimal Guarantee:: The questionnaire remains in Review status if the trained staff member takes no action.

=== Business Rules

[cols="1,4"]
|===
| ID | Rule

| BR-021 | Only questionnaires in Review status can be reviewed.
| BR-022 | Approval, rejection, and change requests require the trained staff member to have viewed the questionnaire.
| BR-023 | Change requests and rejections must include written feedback or a reason.
| BR-024 | A trained staff member may approve a questionnaire even if an automated check has failed, at their discretion.
| BR-025 | Requesting changes transitions the questionnaire back to Draft with feedback attached.
|===

=== Activity Diagram

[plantuml]
----
@startuml
start
:Trained staff opens review dashboard;
:Selects questionnaire in Review status;

:System displays source, preview, and quality check results;

if (Wants patient preview?) then (yes)
  :Render patient preview;
  :Interact with preview;
  :Return to review screen;
else (no)
endif

:Review clinical content;
:Review FHIR mappings;
:Review automated quality check results;

switch (Decision?)
case (Approve)
  :Transition to Approved status;
case (Request Changes)
  :Write feedback;
  :Transition to Draft status;
  :Attach feedback to questionnaire;
case (Reject)
  :Write rejection reason;
  :Transition to Rejected status;
  :Attach reason to questionnaire;
endswitch

stop
@enduml
----

=== Acceptance Criteria

[source,gherkin]
----
Feature: Review a questionnaire

  Background:
    Given a questionnaire "Post-Op Pain Assessment" in Review status
    And automated quality checks have run

  Scenario: Approve a questionnaire
    When the trained staff member opens the questionnaire for review
    Then the system displays the source, rendered preview, and quality check results
    When the trained staff member approves the questionnaire
    Then the questionnaire transitions to Approved status

  Scenario: Request changes
    When the trained staff member opens the questionnaire for review
    And writes feedback "LOINC code on question 2 should be 38208-5"
    And selects Request Changes
    Then the questionnaire transitions to Draft status
    And the feedback is attached to the questionnaire

  Scenario: Reject a questionnaire
    When the trained staff member opens the questionnaire for review
    And writes a rejection reason "Questionnaire duplicates existing assessment"
    And selects Reject
    Then the questionnaire transitions to Rejected status
    And the rejection reason is attached

  Scenario: Approve despite a failed automated check
    Given one automated quality check has failed
    When the trained staff member reviews the questionnaire
    Then the failed check is highlighted
    And the trained staff member can still approve the questionnaire

  Scenario: Preview as patient during review
    When the trained staff member selects patient preview
    Then the questionnaire renders as a patient would see it
    And the trained staff member can interact with the preview
    And no data is stored

  Scenario: Change request or rejection requires feedback
    When the trained staff member selects Request Changes without writing feedback
    Then the system blocks the action
    And prompts for feedback
----

== UC-TS5: Publish a Questionnaire

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| ID | UC-TS5
| Goal | Publish an approved questionnaire so patients can access it
| Actor-Goal Ref | TS-5
| Level | User Goal
| Primary Actor | Trained Staff
|===

=== Stakeholders and Interests

Trained Staff:: Wants to make a reviewed questionnaire available to patients.
Patient:: Gains access to a new questionnaire.
Hospital:: Wants the FHIR Questionnaire resource to exist on the HAPI server for interoperability.

=== Trigger

A questionnaire in Approved status is ready for patient use.

=== Preconditions

. The questionnaire is in Approved status.

=== Main Success Scenario

. The trained staff member opens the questionnaire.
. The trained staff member selects "Publish."
. The system compiles the `.mediform` definition into a FHIR Questionnaire resource. _(SYS-2)_
. The system stores the FHIR Questionnaire on the HAPI FHIR server. _(SYS-6)_
. The system transitions the questionnaire to Published status.
. The system generates a patient-facing link for the questionnaire.
. The system displays the link to the trained staff member.

=== Extensions

*3a. Compilation to FHIR fails.*::
  3a.1. The system displays the compilation errors.
  3a.2. The questionnaire remains in Approved status.
  3a.3. The trained staff member may request changes (returning it to Draft via UC-TS1 extension 7a).

*4a. The HAPI FHIR server is unreachable.*::
  4a.1. The system displays an error.
  4a.2. The questionnaire remains in Approved status.
  4a.3. The trained staff member retries later.

=== Postconditions

Success Guarantee:: The questionnaire is in Published status. A FHIR Questionnaire resource exists on the HAPI server. A patient-facing link is available.
Minimal Guarantee:: The questionnaire remains in Approved status. No FHIR resource is created.

=== Business Rules

[cols="1,4"]
|===
| ID | Rule

| BR-026 | Only questionnaires in Approved status can be published.
| BR-027 | Publishing creates a FHIR Questionnaire resource on the HAPI server.
| BR-028 | Publishing generates a patient-facing link.
| BR-029 | If FHIR compilation fails at publish time, the questionnaire does not transition to Published.
|===

=== Activity Diagram

[plantuml]
----
@startuml
start
:Trained staff opens approved questionnaire;
:Selects Publish;

:Compile .mediform to FHIR Questionnaire;
if (Compilation succeeds?) then (yes)
else (no)
  :Display compilation errors;
  :Questionnaire remains Approved;
  stop
endif

:Store FHIR Questionnaire on HAPI server;
if (Storage succeeds?) then (yes)
else (no)
  :Display error: server unreachable;
  :Questionnaire remains Approved;
  stop
endif

:Transition to Published status;
:Generate patient-facing link;
:Display link to trained staff;

stop
@enduml
----

=== Acceptance Criteria

[source,gherkin]
----
Feature: Publish a questionnaire

  Scenario: Publish an approved questionnaire
    Given a questionnaire in Approved status
    When the trained staff member selects Publish
    Then the system compiles the questionnaire to a FHIR Questionnaire resource
    And stores it on the HAPI FHIR server
    And the questionnaire transitions to Published status
    And a patient-facing link is generated and displayed

  Scenario: Publish fails due to compilation error
    Given a questionnaire in Approved status
    And the .mediform definition has a mapping that cannot compile to FHIR
    When the trained staff member selects Publish
    Then the system displays the compilation error
    And the questionnaire remains in Approved status

  Scenario: Publish fails due to FHIR server unavailability
    Given a questionnaire in Approved status
    And the HAPI FHIR server is unreachable
    When the trained staff member selects Publish
    Then the system displays an error
    And the questionnaire remains in Approved status
    And no FHIR resource is created

  Scenario: Cannot publish a non-approved questionnaire
    Given a questionnaire in Draft status
    Then the Publish action is not available
----

== UC-TS6: Retire a Questionnaire

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| ID | UC-TS6
| Goal | Retire a published questionnaire
| Actor-Goal Ref | TS-6
| Level | User Goal
| Primary Actor | Trained Staff
|===

=== Stakeholders and Interests

Trained Staff:: Wants to stop collecting responses for an outdated or replaced questionnaire.
Hospital:: Wants historical data preserved even after retirement.

=== Trigger

A published questionnaire is no longer needed.

=== Preconditions

. The questionnaire is in Published status.

=== Main Success Scenario

. The trained staff member opens the questionnaire.
. The trained staff member selects "Retire."
. The system asks for confirmation.
. The trained staff member confirms.
. The system transitions the questionnaire to Retired status.
. The system updates the FHIR Questionnaire resource status on the HAPI server to retired.
. The patient-facing link stops accepting new responses.

=== Extensions

*4a. The trained staff member cancels.*::
  4a.1. The questionnaire remains in Published status.

*6a. The HAPI FHIR server is unreachable.*::
  6a.1. The system displays an error.
  6a.2. The questionnaire remains in Published status.

=== Postconditions

Success Guarantee:: The questionnaire is in Retired status. No new responses can be submitted. Existing responses and the FHIR Questionnaire resource are preserved.
Minimal Guarantee:: The questionnaire remains in Published status.

=== Business Rules

[cols="1,4"]
|===
| ID | Rule

| BR-030 | Only questionnaires in Published status can be retired.
| BR-031 | Retirement preserves all existing QuestionnaireResponse and Observation resources.
| BR-032 | Retired questionnaires do not accept new responses.
| BR-033 | Retirement requires confirmation.
|===

=== Activity Diagram

[plantuml]
----
@startuml
start
:Trained staff opens published questionnaire;
:Selects Retire;

:System asks for confirmation;
if (Confirmed?) then (yes)
else (no)
  :Questionnaire remains Published;
  stop
endif

:Update FHIR Questionnaire status to retired;
if (HAPI server reachable?) then (yes)
  :Transition to Retired status;
  :Patient link stops accepting responses;
else (no)
  :Display error;
  :Questionnaire remains Published;
endif

stop
@enduml
----

=== Acceptance Criteria

[source,gherkin]
----
Feature: Retire a questionnaire

  Scenario: Retire a published questionnaire
    Given a questionnaire in Published status
    When the trained staff member selects Retire
    And confirms the action
    Then the questionnaire transitions to Retired status
    And the FHIR Questionnaire resource status is updated to retired
    And the patient-facing link no longer accepts new responses

  Scenario: Cancel retirement
    Given a questionnaire in Published status
    When the trained staff member selects Retire
    And cancels the confirmation
    Then the questionnaire remains in Published status

  Scenario: Existing responses preserved after retirement
    Given a questionnaire in Published status
    And 5 patient responses exist
    When the trained staff member retires the questionnaire
    Then all 5 QuestionnaireResponse resources remain on the HAPI server

  Scenario: Cannot retire a non-published questionnaire
    Given a questionnaire in Draft status
    Then the Retire action is not available
----

== UC-TS7: View Questionnaire Dashboard

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| ID | UC-TS7
| Goal | View a dashboard of questionnaires by status
| Actor-Goal Ref | TS-7
| Level | User Goal
| Primary Actor | Trained Staff
|===

=== Stakeholders and Interests

Trained Staff:: Wants an overview of all questionnaires across the lifecycle to prioritize work.

=== Trigger

The trained staff member opens the employee app.

=== Preconditions

. The trained staff member has access to the employee app.

=== Main Success Scenario

. The trained staff member opens the dashboard.
. The system retrieves all questionnaires across all statuses.
. The system displays the dashboard grouped or filterable by status (Draft, Review, Approved, Published, Retired, Rejected).
. Each entry shows title, status, author (nurse), and last-modified date.
. The trained staff member scans the dashboard to identify questionnaires needing attention.

=== Extensions

*2a. No questionnaires exist.*::
  2a.1. The system displays an empty state.

*5a. The trained staff member filters by status.*::
  5a.1. The system displays only questionnaires matching the selected status.

*5b. The trained staff member opens a questionnaire.*::
  5b.1. The system navigates to the review screen (UC-TS1) or detail view depending on status.

=== Postconditions

Success Guarantee:: The trained staff member sees an accurate, current dashboard. No data is modified.
Minimal Guarantee:: No data is modified.

=== Business Rules

[cols="1,4"]
|===
| ID | Rule

| BR-034 | The dashboard shows all questionnaires, not just those created by the current user.
| BR-035 | Questionnaires in Review status are visually prioritized (they need action).
|===

=== Activity Diagram

[plantuml]
----
@startuml
start
:Trained staff opens dashboard;

:System retrieves all questionnaires;
if (Any questionnaires?) then (yes)
  :Display dashboard grouped by status;
  :Each entry: title, status, author, last-modified;

  if (Filter applied?) then (yes)
    :Show only matching status;
  else (no)
  endif

  if (Opens a questionnaire?) then (yes)
    :Navigate to review or detail view;
  else (no)
  endif
else (no)
  :Display empty state;
endif

stop
@enduml
----

=== Acceptance Criteria

[source,gherkin]
----
Feature: View questionnaire dashboard

  Scenario: View dashboard with questionnaires in various statuses
    Given questionnaires exist in Draft, Review, and Published statuses
    When the trained staff member opens the dashboard
    Then all questionnaires are displayed
    And each entry shows title, status, author, and last-modified date

  Scenario: Filter dashboard by status
    Given questionnaires exist in Draft, Review, and Published statuses
    When the trained staff member filters by Review
    Then only questionnaires in Review status are displayed

  Scenario: Review status is prioritized
    Given questionnaires exist in Review and Draft statuses
    When the trained staff member opens the dashboard
    Then questionnaires in Review status are visually prioritized

  Scenario: Empty dashboard
    Given no questionnaires exist
    When the trained staff member opens the dashboard
    Then the system displays an empty state
----

== UC-TS8: View Quality Check Results

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| ID | UC-TS8
| Goal | View automated quality check results for a questionnaire
| Actor-Goal Ref | TS-8
| Level | User Goal
| Primary Actor | Trained Staff
|===

=== Stakeholders and Interests

Trained Staff:: Wants to understand what automated checks found before making a review decision.

=== Trigger

The trained staff member is reviewing a questionnaire and wants to see quality check details.

=== Preconditions

. The questionnaire is in Review or later status.
. Automated quality checks have run.

=== Main Success Scenario

. The trained staff member opens the quality check results for a questionnaire.
. The system displays each check with its result (pass/fail) and details.
. The trained staff member reviews the results.

=== Extensions

*2a. All checks passed.*::
  2a.1. The system displays a summary: "All checks passed."

*2b. One or more checks failed.*::
  2b.1. The system highlights failed checks with details (which question, what failed, why).

=== Postconditions

Success Guarantee:: The trained staff member has seen the quality check results. No data is modified.
Minimal Guarantee:: No data is modified.

=== Business Rules

[cols="1,4"]
|===
| ID | Rule

| BR-036 | Quality check results are read-only.
| BR-037 | Failed checks display the affected question, the check name, and a description of the failure.
|===

=== Activity Diagram

[plantuml]
----
@startuml
start
:Trained staff opens quality check results;

:System retrieves check results;
:Display each check with pass/fail and details;

if (Any failures?) then (yes)
  :Highlight failed checks;
  :Show affected question, check name, failure description;
else (no)
  :Display summary: all checks passed;
endif

stop
@enduml
----

=== Acceptance Criteria

[source,gherkin]
----
Feature: View quality check results

  Scenario: All checks passed
    Given a questionnaire in Review status
    And all automated quality checks passed
    When the trained staff member views quality check results
    Then the system displays each check as passed
    And shows a summary: all checks passed

  Scenario: Some checks failed
    Given a questionnaire in Review status
    And the check "FHIR codes reference known systems" failed on question 3
    When the trained staff member views quality check results
    Then the failed check is highlighted
    And shows question 3, the check name, and a failure description

  Scenario: Quality check results are read-only
    When the trained staff member views quality check results
    Then no edit or re-run actions are available
----
